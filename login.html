Staff Access

Newsroom workspace for the Kilele team. Access is shift-based: each staff member signs in only within the window allocated by the administrator.

Choose your workspace profile

Preview phase: sign-in is open — no password required. The time window is still enforced, exactly as it will be after full authentication goes live.

How shift-based access works: the Super Admin allocates every user a working window (e.g. 10:00–12:00 EAT) and the sections they may open. Inside the window, the workspace opens. Once the window passes, the session ends automatically and login stops working until the next window. Full Clerk authentication (passwords, secure sessions) is activated at Stage 3 of the project, after contract signing.
