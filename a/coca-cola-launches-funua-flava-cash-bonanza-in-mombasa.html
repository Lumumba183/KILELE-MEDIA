“Coca-Cola Launches ‘Funua Flava’ Cash Bonanza IN MOMBASA”

31 Aug 2025·1 min read·Kilele News Desk

Coca-Cola has officially launched a customer reward campaign known as Funua Flava Pata Mita in Mombasa city. As part of this campaign, customers will have the opportunity to win one million shillings every week.

Speaking at the launch event held at the Mama Ngina Waterfront in Mombasa, Ruth Mwake, a sales officer at the company, stated that customers will also receive various other prizes.

AdvertisementIn-article placement — advertise with Kilele Radio

Customers are required to send the codes found under soda bottle caps to 40111 in order to stand a chance to win those prizes.

Since the official launch of the campaign in the country, many Kenyans have already won a variety of rewards.

Some Mombasa residents who attended today’s launch walked away with cash prizes and other gifts, expressing their joy and excitement.

The campaign will run until November 15 this year.
