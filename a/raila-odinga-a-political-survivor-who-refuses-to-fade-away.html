Raila Odinga: A Political Survivor Who Refuses to Fade Away

18 Feb 2025·4 min read·Kilele News Desk

Addis Ababa – The Unwavering Spirit of Raila Odinga

If there is one defining characteristic of Raila Odinga’s political career, it is his unmatched resilience. For more than three decades, he has weathered numerous electoral defeats, political betrayals, and turbulent shifts in Kenya’s political landscape. Yet, rather than being discouraged, Odinga has consistently used setbacks as stepping stones to his next challenge. His recent failure to secure the African Union Commission (AUC) chairmanship is just another chapter in his long history of political battles.

Many Kenyans viewed this as his golden opportunity to transition from the intense and often divisive local political scene to a diplomatic role on the continental stage. However, fate—and perhaps Odinga’s own unyielding political instincts—had a different plan in store.

A Strategic AU Bid That Fell Short

Odinga’s quest for the AU’s top leadership position was a bold and well-calculated move. His extensive political experience, vast international networks, and deep understanding of governance made him a strong contender for the position.

For his loyal supporters, this bid represented a dignified exit from the grueling and sometimes bruising world of Kenyan politics. It was an opportunity for Odinga to leave behind the relentless political contests and take up a statesman’s role at the continental level. However, the political dynamics within the African Union proved challenging. Internal negotiations, shifting alliances, and behind-the-scenes power plays all contributed to derailing his campaign. In the end, despite his credentials and experience, he found himself on the losing side once again.

While some viewed this loss as yet another instance of political misfortune, others saw it as a testament to the complexities of African diplomacy. Even beyond Kenya, Odinga’s political path continues to be shaped by unpredictable twists and turns.

Mixed Reactions from Kenyans

News of Odinga’s unsuccessful bid sparked varied reactions across Kenya. His devoted followers were disheartened, seeing the outcome as another instance of their leader being denied a well-deserved victory. To them, his loss represented an injustice that mirrored previous disappointments in Kenya’s political arena.

On the other hand, a different segment of Kenyans reacted with indifference or even quiet relief. Some had already begun entertaining the prospect of a political landscape without Odinga’s dominant presence, hoping that his new role at the AU would offer a fresh start for Kenyan politics. There were even speculative theories suggesting that the Kenyan government may have subtly worked against his bid, preferring to keep him entangled in domestic politics rather than allowing him to take up a continental leadership role.

But if Kenyans have learned anything over the years, it is that Raila Odinga does not simply fade into the background.

What Next for Odinga?

Even before the dust settled on his AU bid, discussions were already underway about his next political move. Will he resume his role as a vocal opposition leader, challenging President William Ruto’s administration? Will he reawaken his protest strategies, mobilizing supporters in a bid to demand political accountability? Or will he set his sights on yet another ambitious pursuit—perhaps even a sixth presidential campaign?

One thing remains clear: Odinga is unlikely to retire quietly. His political instincts, coupled with his deep influence in Kenya’s political landscape, make it nearly impossible for him to take a backseat. By the time Kenyans fully process his latest setback, he will likely have already repositioned himself for the next major political battle.

A Legacy of Resilience

Odinga’s ability to transform defeat into momentum is both remarkable and, to his critics, exhausting. Where many political figures withdraw after repeated losses, he finds renewed purpose in every setback. His loss in the AU race may have ended his continental ambitions for now, but it has simultaneously reignited his presence in the local political sphere.

If history is any indication, it will not be long before he is back in the public eye—calling for reforms, galvanizing his base, and reinforcing his position as one of Kenya’s most formidable political figures.

The Inevitable Return of Baba

Ultimately, Raila Odinga is a man who refuses to be defined by defeat. For him, every loss is merely a setup for his next comeback. Whether admired or opposed, there is no denying that his influence in Kenyan politics is as constant as the election cycles themselves. While some had envisioned his AU bid as a graceful exit, it appears that Odinga is here to stay—ready for yet another round of political battles.
