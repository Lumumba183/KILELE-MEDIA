Moses Kuria Resigns from Ruto’s Administration, Citing Personal Reasons Amid Political Speculation

09 Jul 2025·2 min read·Kilele News Desk

In a unexpected development, Moses Kuria, the Senior Economic Advisor to President William Ruto, announced his resignation from the Kenya Kwanza government on July 8, 2025. The news, disseminated through a statement on his verified X account, has ignited a flurry of speculation regarding the underlying reasons for his departure and its potential impact on Kenya’s political scene.

Kuria, known for his forthright demeanor, revealed that he had a meeting with President Ruto, who accepted his decision to step down. Looking back on his time in office, Kuria acknowledged his roles over the past two years, which included serving as Cabinet Secretary for Investments, Trade, and Industry for 11 months, Cabinet Secretary for Public Service, Delivery, and Performance Management for 9 months, and Senior Economic Advisor for the last 10 months. “I am thankful for the chance to work on the Bottom-Up Economic Transformation Agenda, a key component of President Ruto’s 2022 campaign platform,” he noted, attributing his resignation to personal pursuits.

This announcement follows closely on the heels of a controversial X post by Kuria, where he alluded to the possibility of “no elections in 2027.” The remark drew sharp criticism and raised questions about whether it was a personal opinion or indicative of a larger governmental plan. Although Kuria did not explicitly connect his resignation to the backlash, political commentators believe the public reaction might have influenced his choice to leave.

Kuria’s exit is significant for the Kenya Kwanza administration, which has been grappling with governance issues and public demonstrations recently. As a central figure in crafting the government’s economic strategies, his departure prompts concerns about the administration’s future trajectory, especially in the influential Mount Kenya region where Kuria holds considerable sway.

There is much conjecture about Kuria’s next steps, with some suggesting he might be preparing for a political resurgence before the 2027 elections. Rumors of potential alliances with opposition leaders or efforts to strengthen his base in the Mount Kenya region have emerged, though no concrete details have been confirmed. The State House has not issued any statements regarding Kuria’s resignation, leaving his future intentions ambiguous.

As Kenya continues to navigate a intricate political and economic environment, Kuria’s resignation represents a critical juncture, with observers closely watching how this shift will influence the nation’s political landscape in the coming months.
